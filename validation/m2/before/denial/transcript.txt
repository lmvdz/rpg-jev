THE GILDED CARP

You came in out of the rain this afternoon, paid for a bed, and slept. When you came down at
dusk the ford had gone under and the mood in the common room had gone with it. The inn's
account ledger is missing from the office. The crown's assessor arrives at first light to
read it, and an inn that cannot show its ledger is taxed at his pleasure.

Nobody has said it to your face yet. But you were the only stranger in the house, and the
innkeeper has not taken her eyes off you since you sat down.

(Type help for commands. Plain verbs always work; anything else, say it as you would.)

The common room: a low ceiling black with smoke, a long bar, a hearth losing its fight with the damp. Rain ticks at the shutters. The office door stands beside the stairs, an arch leads to the kitchen, and the yard door rattles in its frame.
Mara is here, tending the bar.
You see a pewter tankard and a heel of bread.
From here: the kitchen, the stable yard and the office (locked).

> tell mara I never touched her ledger
You tell Mara, plainly, that you never touched the ledger.
Mara says: "I'll say it plain. I say you took my ledger at dusk."

> tell mara I was sitting right here in the common room at dusk
You tell Mara that you were sitting in the common room in plain view of everyone.
Mara says: "Who saw you there? Name one."

> tell mara it was odo who took it
You tell Mara that Odo took the ledger.
Mara says nothing. Her eyes do not leave your hands.

> wait 10
You wait. (19:19)

> talk to mara
You greet Mara.
Mara says: "At dusk. Where were you? Think before you answer."

> wait 60
You wait. (20:22)
Tobin comes in from the kitchen, bringing in firewood.
Mara says to Tobin: "Hear this, then. I saw it myself: the stranger accused Odo of taking my ledger an hour or so ago."
Tobin goes out toward the stable yard.

> wait 60
You wait. (21:22)
Mara goes out toward the kitchen.
Odo comes in from the kitchen, carrying bowls of stew round the tables.
Mara comes in from the kitchen, tending the bar.
Tobin comes in from the stable yard, bringing in firewood.
Mara says to Odo: "I saw it myself: the stranger accused you of taking my ledger an hour or so ago."
Odo says, grinning: "Funny thing. I say you were hanging about the office at dusk. I only mention it."
Tobin goes out toward the stable yard.
Odo goes out toward the kitchen.

> wait 60
You wait. (22:22)
Mara says: "Turn out your pack. Now, on the table, where I can see it."
You turn out your pack on the nearest table: a spare shirt, a heel of bread, a whetstone. It is gone through twice, and nothing in it is anyone else's.
Tobin comes in from the stable yard, bringing in firewood.
Mara says to Tobin: "I saw it myself: the stranger had nothing of the inn's in their pack when it was searched an hour or so ago."

> wait 60
You wait. (23:22)
Tobin goes out toward the stable yard.
Mara goes out toward the kitchen.
Mara comes in from the kitchen, tending the bar.
Tobin comes in from the stable yard, bringing in firewood.
Mara says to Tobin: "Hear this, then. I say the stranger took my ledger at dusk."
Tobin goes out toward the stable yard.

> wait 60
You wait. (0:22)
Mara sends Tobin out into the rain with a lantern and a message for the constable at the mill. "You'll stay where I can see you until he comes," she says, and sets a stool by the door with a cudgel across her knees. It is a long night.

*** You are taken for the thief. ***
